Have2Run Data Deletion Guide

Last Updated: February 8, 2026

한국어

1. Overview

Have2Run respects your right to data deletion. You can delete all data associated with your account at any time.

2. In-App Account Deletion

You can delete your account directly from the app:

  1. Open the Have2Run app
  2. Go to the More tab
  3. Tap Account Management
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion in the dialog

Account deletion is processed immediately and cannot be undone.

3. Data That Gets Deleted

The following data is permanently deleted when you delete your account:

Data Type Details Deletion Timing
Profile Information Name, email, height, weight, personal records Immediate
Training Data Training sessions, schedules, completion records Immediate
AI Coaching Conversations Chat history with AI coach Immediate
Watch Sync Data Running records imported from smartwatch Immediate
Uploaded Images Workout screenshots, profile photos Immediate
Authentication Data Account login information Immediate
Analytics Data Anonymized usage patterns (Firebase Analytics) Up to 60 days

4. Request Deletion via Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request data deletion via email:

  • Email: privacy@have2run.com
  • Subject: [Data Deletion Request] Have2Run Account Deletion
  • Required information: Email address used for registration

Email requests are processed within 3 business days.

5. Health Data

Have2Run only reads data from HealthKit (iOS) and Health Connect (Android). Deleting your Have2Run data does not affect the original data stored in your device's health app.

To manage health data on your device:

  • iOS: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Have2Run
  • Android: Settings > Health Connect > App Permissions > Have2Run

6. Third-Party Service Data

When you delete your account, all data on Have2Run servers is deleted. Social login (Google, Apple, Kakao) provider connections should be separately revoked in each service's settings.

7. Contact

For additional questions about data deletion:

  • Email: privacy@have2run.com
