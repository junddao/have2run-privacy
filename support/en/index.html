Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Account

Q. I forgot my password

Please contact support@have2run.com with the email address you signed up with. If you signed up with Google, Apple, or Kakao, simply sign in with that account again.

Q. I want to delete my account

Go to the Settings tab and tap Delete Account at the bottom. All your data, including shoes, run records, uploaded shoe photos, and your Strava connection, is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. For details, see Data Deletion.

Q. I want to change my social login account

Changing your social login account is not currently supported. To use a different account, please delete your existing account and sign up again.

Run Detection

Q. How does run detection work?

When you finish a run with your watch (Garmin, Suunto, etc.), the watch maker's app saves it to Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android). Have2Run detects it and sends a "Which shoes did you wear?" notification. Tap it, pick a shoe, and confirm to record the run. You can also use Strava for detection instead of your watch.

Only one detection source can be active at a time: Apple Health, Health Connect, or Strava. Turning one on turns the others off. You can switch in Settings > Watch Sync. Runs whose notification you ignore are not recorded, so add them manually if needed.

Q. I ran but didn't get a notification

Please check the following:

  1. In your watch maker's app (Garmin Connect, Suunto, etc.), Apple Health / Health Connect sharing is on and the run has synced to the watch app
  2. iOS: Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Have2Run allows reading workouts and distance
  3. Android: Health Connect > App permissions > Have2Run allows reading exercise and distance, plus access data in the background (Android checks for new runs about every 15 minutes)
  4. If you use Strava: Strava is connected in the Settings tab and the activity has been uploaded to Strava
  5. Notifications for Have2Run are allowed in your device settings

The connection guide under Settings > Watch Sync can check whether your recent runs are being detected.

Q. How do I record a run manually?

In the Records tab, tap Add Run and enter the date, distance, and shoe. You can also tap a date in the Records tab to edit or delete that day's runs.

Shoes

Q. What does "명분 완성" (Excuse Unlocked) mean?

When a shoe reaches its lifespan distance, we celebrate with "명분 완성! 새 신발 사도 돼요 🎉" — you've earned a reason to buy new shoes. In Have2Run, replacing shoes isn't a warning; it's a moment to celebrate. Retire the shoe from its detail screen to move it to the Hall of Fame (retired shoes) and register your new pair right away.

Q. The shoe illustration doesn't match my shoe

Catalog illustrations are generated automatically from public product information and may differ from the real shoe. Open the shoe's detail screen, tap the menu at the top right, and choose 다른 신발 같아요 (Looks like a different shoe). Once enough reports come in, the illustration is regenerated. You can also replace it with your own photo.

Q. How do I change a shoe's lifespan?

Use the menu at the top right of the shoe's detail screen to change its lifespan distance. The default lifespan for new shoes is set in Settings > Default Lifespan.
