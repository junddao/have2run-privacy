Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Account

Q. I forgot my password

Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen and enter the email you used to sign up. A password reset link will be sent to your email.

Q. I want to delete my account

You can delete your account at More tab > Account Management > Delete Account. All data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. See our Data Deletion Guide for details.

Q. I want to change my social login account

Changing social login accounts is not currently supported. To use a different account, please delete your existing account and sign up again.

AI Coaching

Q. The AI coach is not responding

Please check your network connection. AI coaching requires an internet connection. If the issue persists, restart the app or contact support@have2run.com.

Q. The AI coach's training plan doesn't suit me

Make sure your current fitness level and goals are accurately set in your profile. You can ask the AI coach directly, like "lower the training intensity" or "give me an easier plan" for personalized adjustments.

Watch Sync

Q. Watch data is not syncing

Please check the following:

  1. Make sure sync is enabled in More > Settings > Watch Sync
  2. iOS: Check that Have2Run has permission in Settings > Health > Data Access
  3. Android: Check that Have2Run has permission in Settings > Health Connect
  4. Verify that running data is recorded in your device's health app

Q. I want to turn off watch sync

You can disable the sync toggle in More tab > Settings > Watch Sync. Turning off sync stops new data collection.

Training Management

Q. I completed my training but it wasn't recorded

You can record training completion through:

  • Tell the AI coach "I completed today's training"
  • Automatic smartwatch sync (when watch sync is enabled)
  • Send a workout screenshot to the AI coach

Q. I want to edit a training record

Just tell the AI coach "edit yesterday's training record." You can modify distance, time, pace, and other details.

Bug Reports

Found a bug or error in the app? Please send the following information to support@have2run.com:

  • Device model (e.g., iPhone 15, Galaxy S24)
  • App version (visible at the bottom of the More tab)
  • Description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if possible)
