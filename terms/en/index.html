2. Service Features

Have2Run provides the following features:

  • Shoe Rack: Register your running shoes and see the total distance and remaining lifespan of each pair
  • Automatic Run Detection: Detect runs finished via HealthKit/Health Connect or Strava (optional), notify you, and record them on the shoe you choose
  • Manual Entry: Record date, distance, and shoe yourself without any connection
  • Run Calendar: Runs by date and monthly summaries
  • Shoe Catalog: Search by brand and model, with automatically generated shoe illustrations
  • Replacement Reminders & Retirement: Alerts when a shoe nears the end of its lifespan, and a place for retired shoes
